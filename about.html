THE INTRO

Hi, I’m Vea! ☺︎

“Every story is a playlist of moments, and every moment adds a new beat to who we become.”

I’m Vea Joyce Ringor, a student currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Criminology (BSCRIM) at the University of the Cordilleras in Baguio City. Born on November 30, 2006, and raised in Taguig City, Metro Manila, my journey has been shaped by the places I’ve called home, the lessons I’ve learned, and the experiences that continue to help me grow.

I believe the best stories are built one small moment at a time. As a student, I’m discovering my voice through the things I learn, create, and care about. This website is my evolving scrapbook—a personal playlist of my passions, dreams, goals, and experiences that are shaping the person I am and the person I hope to become.
